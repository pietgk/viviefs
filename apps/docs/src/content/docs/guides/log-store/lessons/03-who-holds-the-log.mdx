</details>

## The idea

**A device cannot keep everything forever, but someone may still need it.**
A sync peer, or a trace sink exporting spans, reads the log from its own
position. Removing facts it has not read loses them for good.

**Consumers say what they have seen.** A consumer is named (`device-a`,
`trace/motel`) and calls `acknowledge(consumer, cursor)` after it has really
delivered the facts up to `cursor`. `acknowledged(consumer)` reads that
position back.

**The horizon is the slowest consumer.** `compact()` removes only datoms at
or below the lowest acknowledged cursor. When a consumer stays behind, the log
stays too, and `compact()` names it in `heldBy`, so a sink that is down shows
up as the reason instead of as missing spans.

<Sample path="libs/datom/src/log-store.ts" region="compact" />

**To let compaction go on, catch the slow consumer up.** Read from its own
cursor, deliver, acknowledge the last `seq` delivered, then compact.
Acknowledging first and delivering after looks the same until a crash in
between: then compaction removes facts that were never delivered.

**The server never compacts.** Its log is the audit trail
(ADR-0011); only device logs compact.
