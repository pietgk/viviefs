A lesson is one page in `lessons/NN-name.mdx` that teaches one idea and ends
in exercise section `NN` (D81). Keep it short enough to hold in working
memory. Frontmatter: `lesson` is `NN` as a number, `evidence` lists the
evidence notes it cites (at least one, under `docs/evidence/`), `source` is
its one primary source.

## Recall first

Two or three questions on the lesson before, or on the concepts this one
needs. Fold each answer in a `<details>` block, so the reader tries first.

## The idea

The one idea, step by step, with the exemplar's own code embedded by region.

## What the gate showed

The claim, the gate that established it and its evidence note. This page
explains; the gate proves.

## Your turn

What exercise `NN.MM` asks, in one or two sentences, and what passing means.
Then the same three steps in every lesson (D91):

1. Run it: `pnpm exercise <pattern> NN.MM`. The first run is red on purpose:
   `problem/` runs, but does not yet do what this lesson teaches, and the
   verdict says what is missing.
2. Edit the `problem/` file, named as a path, and run it again until the
   verdict says done.
3. Compare with the `solution/` file, then answer the questions below.

## Check yourself

Two or three questions, answers folded, each answer about as long as the
others so their shape gives nothing away.
