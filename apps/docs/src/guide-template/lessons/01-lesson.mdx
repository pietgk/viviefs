A lesson is one page in `lessons/NN-name.mdx` that teaches one idea and ends
in exercise section `NN` ([D81 What a lesson is][D81]). Keep it short enough to hold in working
memory. Frontmatter: `lesson` is `NN` as a number, `evidence` lists the
evidence notes it cites (at least one, under `docs/evidence/`), `source` is
its one primary source, and `widget` names its widget's model
([D216 Every lesson shows its idea][D216]).

## Recall first

Two or three questions on the lesson before, or on the concepts this one
needs. Fold each answer in a `<details>` block, so the reader tries first.

## The idea

The one idea in one or two sentences, then shown before it is named
([D216 Every lesson shows its idea][D216]):

- At least one diagram: a Mermaid sequence diagram for who calls whom, a state
  diagram for what something goes through, a flowchart for how a choice is
  made. Its labels follow the rules prose follows.
- A `### Try it` section with a widget the learner operates. Its model is a
  pure module in `apps/docs/src/lib`, named in `widget:`, and
  `<model>.integration.test.ts` beside it plays the widget's scenarios on the
  real code, so the widget cannot teach what the exemplar does not do. Its
  controls follow the convention at the top of `apps/docs/src/styles/widget.css`:
  a choice is a form control, a button is an action.

Then what the learner saw, the words for it as cards read from the glossary,
and the concept's rule, embedded from its one home in `apps/docs/src/partials/`
and never written again here ([D221 One home for every piece of knowledge][D221]). Code samples name the
Effect ideas they use ([Before you start](/guides/before-you-start/)).

## What the gate showed

The claim, the gate that established it and its evidence note. This page
explains; the gate proves.

## Your turn

What exercise `NN.MM` asks, in one or two sentences, then
`<Exercise pattern="<pattern>" id="NN.MM" />`. It shows the problem, what its
run prints, the change to the solution as a diff, and what the solution's run
prints, so the exercise is reviewed, as code is ([D222 Your turn is a review][D222]). The runs come
from `exercises/<pattern>/runs.json`, which `pnpm exercise:record` writes and
`verify` keeps equal to a fresh run. The learner can still run
`pnpm exercise <pattern> NN.MM` ([D91 The exercise loop][D91]).

## Check yourself

Two or three questions, answers folded, each answer about as long as the
others so their shape gives nothing away.
