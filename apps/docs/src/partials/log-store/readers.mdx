Every fact gets `seq`, its position in this store's log, and a reader of the
log keeps its own place as a position. `streamFrom(place, limit)` returns the
facts after a place, in log order, at most `limit` of them. The read models,
trace export and sync all read a log this way, each from its own place and at
its own pace.

<Sample path="libs/datom/src/log-store.ts" region="stream-from" />

A reader that keeps its place in the log store is a consumer. It delivers
what it read, then records the last position with `acknowledge(consumer, seq)`,
and after a restart `acknowledged(consumer)` says where to read on. Trace
export does this for each sink as `trace/<sink>`; the read models keep their
place the same way in their own table, and a device keeps its place in the
server's log as its sync cursor.

Deliver, then acknowledge. A crash in between only delivers the same facts
again, and the receiver can tell, because a fact sent again has the same
`tx`. Acknowledge first, and a crash moves the place past facts that were
never delivered, with nothing to tell. Trace export, as the code runs it:

<Sample path="libs/telemetry/src/trace-projector.ts" region="export-once" />

A place is a position in this store's log: the same fact has another `seq`
in another replica's log, so a place means nothing elsewhere. Acknowledging
never removes a fact, and the log store has no call that does
([ADR-0011 Log store pattern, read models, compaction and analytics][ADR-0011]). What a device may one day remove is decided with sync
([I107 Device compaction][I107]).
