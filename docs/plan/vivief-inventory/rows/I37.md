## Notes

- **Where the two stacks meet and part.** ViViEfs already runs much of the framework under other names (I34's table): decisions with reasons, then ADRs; gates in run order for phases; `Decide when:` for deferral triggers; verify for the deterministic build pipeline; positive controls for rule validation. They part on order and on agents. vivief's KB builds the core single-user first and syncs last (phase 20+); ViViEfs proves sync in its foundation (P09). And the KB's deterministic-first loop (triggers, proposals with confidence and evidence, review, refinement, auto-promotion, trust scores) has no mechanism here: a person runs `retro` and decides, and nothing measures whether a written rule helps an agent.
