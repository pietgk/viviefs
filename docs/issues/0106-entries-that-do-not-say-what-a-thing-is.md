# I106: Entries that do not say what a thing is

Status: needs-triage

Category: enhancement

Decide when: the human has read lesson 1 on the site; before any guide teaches Device, Workflow runner or Pattern

Found: 2026-11-10

## What

The glossary entries for Device, Workflow runner and Pattern do not say what each thing essentially is, and the human named this a deeper issue than the entry format. A line in effect form could not be written for any of the three without it describing another word: Device came out as a field of the envelope, Workflow runner as what the workflow engine does, and Pattern as what Delivered means.

This showed in the appendix of the second lesson 1 prototype, which wrote [I30 Ideas batch: glossary entries written as effects][I30]'s effect form for 15 terms of every kind: 7 fit (operations and states), 5 appear inside another term's line (nouns), and 3 were forced (these three, a role, a role and a project word). The human read the result in two ways:

- **A line that will not come out says the text needs work.** Writing the line is a test of the entry, whether or not the line is kept.
- **If we cannot describe Device, Workflow runner and Pattern by their essential parts, the gap is in our understanding, not in the format.** All three are central: a device is where an app runs and keeps its local replica, a workflow runner is what lets durable work run on a phone and on the server, and the pattern is the unit this repository delivers.

What a fix looks like is not decided. One way: for each of the three, write what it holds, what it does, and what would be missing without it, before writing its sentence again.
