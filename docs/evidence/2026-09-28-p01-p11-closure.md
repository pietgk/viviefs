## What it took

`pnpm qualify` runs every registered gate, and P12-P14 refuse by design, so
the harness gained `--through Pnn`: every gate in order up to `Pnn`, with the
same committed-tree checks (`46e9c22b6`).

Four earlier runs on the same evening each failed one or two gates. Every
failure was in the lab or the harness, not in a gate's claim, and every gate
passed in at least two of the four:

| Run | Commit | Failed | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cb9d020a` | `46e9c22b` | P11 on Android | P07 opens the notification shade to tap its notification and left it open; P11's sign-in button was behind it. P07's tap never reached the device because the agent-device daemon, started by an iOS call, had no `adb` on its PATH | `ec775f86e`: P07 closes the shade, every Android session and relaunch closes it first, and every agent-device call carries the Android SDK on PATH |
| `3d098d65` | `ec775f86` | P07, P10 | motel answered `/api/health` and search, but every `POST /v1/traces` hung. The daemon had run for eight days from a `/tmp` copy that macOS's periodic cleanup had emptied, and the harness reused it because health said ok | `d592d67e0`: a running motel is reused only if it stores a canary span; otherwise the pinned one from `vendor/motel` is started and must store it too |
| `055a104c` | `d592d67e` | P11 on iOS | Right after bob's sign-in sheet closed, agent-device's runner logged "Could not match active AX application for XCTest application"; the press saw an empty tree | `4d192db41`: a P11 tap retries only a selector miss, every 5 s for up to 60 s |
| `88ba777c` | `4d192db4` | P02 on Android | `agent-cli navigate` was killed at 60 s with no report; logcat shows the app on the dev launcher's home screen with no bundle loaded. navigate itself waits up to 45 s twice and then reopens the app once | `59b74a9a2`: navigate gets 240 s, so its own recovery and its explanation are not cut off |

Each fix was reproduced before it was written where the failure could be
triggered: the shade (opened by hand before a P11 Android run) and the hung
motel (the daemon itself). The iOS accessibility miss and the navigate stall
could not be triggered on demand; their evidence is agent-device's runner log
and the emulator's logcat.
