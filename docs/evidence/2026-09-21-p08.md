## Comparison

Type safety: effect-machine and XState keep impossible phases out of the
state tree; Atom uses a tagged `ComposerEvent` union plus a reducer that
refuses illegal transitions. effect-machine keeps Effect errors typed;
XState's `fromPromise` erases them into Promise rejection. `candidateHash`
exists only on reviewing and submitting in the effect-machine schema.

The teaching graph is generated from the same `intentComposerGraph` the
effect-machine handle reads. A unit test diffs it against the committed
mermaid file.

Product-specific criteria (voice, affordance) stayed out (D55). No OTLP on
the composer.
