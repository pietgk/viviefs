Round 1: the destination

Q1 - What is the destination?

Map 1 overrode "plan, don't do" because its record was the deliverable. Map 2 is the first map that decides, so the default holds: tickets end in decisions, recorded where this repo keeps decisions (domain docs: the decision log first, an ADR when architectural, the glossary inline). The question is what proves the map is done.

Q2 - Must every one of the 101 rows get an answer?

About 40 rows ask nothing (ViViEfs mechanisms such as the hybrid logical clock or drain before close) and 14 are taken. A cluster decision usually answers many rows at once: if Projection stays several patterns, the six Projection rows that ask "one thing or several?" are answered together.

Q3 - What is the map called?

A clash. "Foundation" already means something here: P01-P10 are the foundation gates, D46 named six foundation themes, and the foundation closed on 2026-09-26 (qualification gates, open items). A map called Foundation, and a glossary term "foundation concept", would give the word two meanings. I34 used it before that was noticed.

Q4 - Does map 2 decide what ViViEfs is about?

I34 offers three readings: creation (vivief's thesis), "creation and communication, with teaching, using learning as the verification", or something narrower that fits a reference stack. Today the glossary says ViViEfs is "a worked example of how to build a phone and web app together with its server, so later apps can copy what is proven here". That answer frames everything else on the map: whether vivief's five concepts are a closed set here, whether "the loop at every scale" names our verify-gate-guide loops.

Q5 - Who writes the names: map 2 or map 3?

Map 3 (Vocabulary) owns the words with two meanings (contract, effectHandler, intent, effect). Domain modeling writes the glossary the moment a term is resolved. If map 2 adopts vivief's Projection as one concept, what goes in the glossary that day?

Q6 - Where does map 2 stop?

A decision such as "Projection is one concept" has consequences: a guide restructure, a lib layout (vivief:concept-aligned-packages), an ADR amendment with a gate. Decisions are accepted intent, unverified until their named gate.
