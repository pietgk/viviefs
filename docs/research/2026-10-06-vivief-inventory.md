# The vivief inventory

Recorded 2026-10-06 by [I45: Assemble the vivief inventory](../issues/0045-assemble-the-vivief-inventory.md),
the record [the vivief inventory map](../plan/vivief-inventory/map.md) finds its way to. For every idea in vivief
and in ViViEfs it says where the idea stands in the other. It is assembled from the ten rows files
`docs/plan/vivief-inventory/rows/I35.md` to `I44.md`, which stay as the trail: each row here names the slices
that wrote it, and their files hold the full citations, coverage and notes. The
[HTML view](2026-10-06-vivief-inventory.html) shows the same rows by map with filters.
[I46: Review the vivief inventory](../issues/0046-review-the-vivief-inventory.md) walked it with the human, who
accepted it on 2026-10-06 with the corrections in Review (I46). It records and asks; it decides nothing, which is the work of maps 2-6 of
[I34](../issues/0034-vivief-as-the-source-of-viviefs-concepts.md).

## How to read a row

The columns, statuses, levels and scopes are the map's (its Notes, "Where an idea stands"). In short:

- **Id**: `vivief:<slug>` (an idea of vivief, from I35 or a slice) or `viviefs:<slug>` (an idea of ViViEfs,
  from I36). A row that holds one idea named twice carries every id it absorbed, the index id first.
- **vivief** and **ViViEfs**: the citations on each side. Where several slices wrote the row, each slice's
  vivief citations are kept whole, marked with the slice (**I37** kb §6 ...). vivief's own words are in
  code font; vivief's ADRs are written `vivief ADR 0051`, never as ViViEfs's `ADR-nnnn`. A ViViEfs row that no
  slice matched names its nearest vivief rows (`Nearest:`), each with that row's status and first citation.
- **Status**: `taken`, `different`, `deferred`, `missing`, `rejected` or `ViViEfs only`.
- **Level** (for `taken`, `different` and `ViViEfs only`): `accepted`, `proposed`, `implemented` or
  `verified (Pnn)`, the last only where `tools/qualification/gate-ledger.json` records a pass.
- **Scope**: `reference stack`, `product vision` or `DevAC tooling`. Only reference-stack rows have a map and a
  question.
- **Map**: 2 Foundation, 3 Vocabulary, 4 From concept to the first exemplar, 5 Showing and understanding,
  6 Agents and trust; the first is where the row is listed.
- **Apps**: for a reference-stack row, the candidate consumer apps that need the idea, each with the source that
  says so ([I9](../issues/0009-product-apps-as-app-pairs.md) Comments name them: BirVana, ERP, GRC, counseling,
  procurement and probably retail). The unit is the row's idea, not a feature: whether an app needs ideas or
  features is a question map 4 inherits (Review, below). Counseling and procurement needs come from vivief's own
  product contracts and intents (their abbreviations: `cp` the counseling contract, `pr`, `pts`, `pep`,
  `mvp01`-`mvp05` the procurement contracts, `ac`, `ip`, `pp`, `sop` the procurement intents, `ev` the evolution
  logs); BirVana, ERP and GRC needs from ViViEfs's decisions, ADRs and bootstrap research; retail has no source
  yet that says what it needs, so it appears nowhere.
- **Question**: what the map that owns the row must decide; several are numbered when slices asked several.
- **Rows**: the slices that wrote the row; their rows files are `docs/plan/vivief-inventory/rows/I<n>.md`.
