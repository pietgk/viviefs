# XDevl

Vocabulary for this living research area. It names the parts of the area.
App creation, Pattern, Guide, Claim, Decision, Verify and Evidence note mean
what the [repository glossary](../../../GLOSSARY.md) says. They are not
redefined here.

## The account

**Our way**:
The account this area keeps of developing software by giving the human, the
LLM and the system each the work it is strongest at.
_Avoid_: process, methodology, playbook

**Stable core**:
The short part of our way that only a promotion may change.
_Avoid_: spec, canon, guide

**Practice**:
A sentence in the stable core, in EARS or Given/When/Then, that names who
verifies it and its evidence label.
_Avoid_: pattern, rule, guideline

**Snapshot**:
A dated research record kept as it was written.
_Avoid_: draft, copy

## The loop

**Insight**:
A dated statement in the inbox, with a source, an evidence label, and the
practice it would affect.
_Avoid_: finding, note, idea

**Grill session**:
The conversation that decides one insight's grill outcome. It is the only
way into the stable core.
_Avoid_: review, meeting, chat

**Grill outcome**:
What a grill session decides for one insight: promote, park, or reject.
_Avoid_: decision, verdict

**Promotion**:
A grill outcome a human has approved, after which the stable core carries
the statement.
_Avoid_: merge, landing

**Park**:
A grill outcome that leaves an insight out of the stable core for now, with
a reason.
_Avoid_: defer, postpone

**Reject**:
A grill outcome that leaves an insight out of the stable core because it
does not hold, with a reason.
_Avoid_: delete, discard

**Reason**:
The sentence recorded with a grill outcome that says why.
_Avoid_: rationale

## Who does the work

**Human**:
The person who approves a promotion. In this area, that person is Piet.
_Avoid_: user, owner

**LLM**:
A language model in a named role: the Research bot, XDevl, or a coding agent.
_Avoid_: AI, model

**Research bot**:
The LLM role that adds a snapshot and appends an insight, and leaves the
stable core unchanged.
_Avoid_: agent, researcher

**XDevl**:
The LLM role that grills with the human.
_Avoid_: assistant

**Coding agent**:
The LLM role that edits the repository under the stable core.
_Avoid_: agent, coder

**System**:
The checks and files that hold the work: what verify runs, what the docs
site shows, and what a skill requires.
_Avoid_: tooling, platform

## Evidence

**Evidence label**:
The mark on an insight that says what the statement rests on: verified,
release, X chatter, our experiment, or unlabelled.
_Avoid_: proof, qualified, evidence note
